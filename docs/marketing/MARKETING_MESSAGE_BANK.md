# Marketing Message Bank

**Status:** **Living asset — NOT authoritative for product truth.** Owner: Founder (Stephen).
**Category:** Marketing.

This is a working bank of lines, hooks, angles, series concepts, and campaign ideas. It is
meant to be added to constantly and never finished.

> ## The one rule that outranks everything else in this file
>
> **A line's status flag is not a suggestion. It is the difference between marketing and a
> false claim.**
>
> Nothing in here may be published in a way that implies an unbuilt capability is available
> today. A great line about a feature that does not exist is a great line **for later**.

## Status labels

Every entry carries exactly one.

| Flag | Meaning | May be published as-is? |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **SHIPPED / SAFE TO MARKET** | The capability exists on `main` and behaves as the line implies | **Yes** |
| 🟡 **PLANNED / DO NOT PROMISE YET** | Approved direction or previewed in-app, but not usable | **Only** as explicitly future-tense: "we're building", "coming", "what we want to do" |
| 🔴 **EXPLORATORY / CONCEPT ONLY** | Being thought about; no decision, no build | **Only** as opinion or vision, never as a feature. Never with a date |

**Third-flag discipline.** 🔴 lines are the most quotable in the bank and the most dangerous.
"If you honor the booking, The Book protects you" is a *belief about how a marketplace should
work*. Said in a founder-POV post it is honest. Said on a landing page it is a promise the
product cannot currently keep.

**A 🟡 or 🔴 line never gets a date attached.** "Coming soon" is a posture; "coming in
March" is a commitment, and this bank does not hold commitments.

## Where the status flags come from

Product truth lives in [../product/BETA_SCOPE.md](../product/BETA_SCOPE.md) (what each
surface actually is: REAL / PARTIAL / PLACEHOLDER / DEFERRED / UNDECIDED),
[../product/CURRENT_STATE.md](../product/CURRENT_STATE.md) (what is true on `main`), and
[../product/PRODUCT_DECISIONS.md](../product/PRODUCT_DECISIONS.md) (locked decisions).

**When they disagree with this file, they win, and this file gets corrected.** A flag here is
a claim about the product and goes stale the same way any other claim does.

**Re-check flags before any campaign.** A 🟡 can become 🟢; a 🟢 can become wrong if a
capability is narrowed. Nothing in this bank ages automatically.

## The workflow rule this file is half of

When product work turns up something worth saying out loud:

1. **Product implication** → the right product doc:
   [FUTURE_PRODUCT_IDEAS.md](../product/FUTURE_PRODUCT_IDEAS.md) if exploratory,
   [OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](../product/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) if deliberately undecided,
   [ROADMAP.md](../product/ROADMAP.md) if approved and being sequenced.
2. **Marketing implication** → this file, with a status flag.
3. **Marketing copy never promotes an exploratory or unbuilt capability as live.**
4. **A Product Decision is never created from marketing copy.** A line landing well is
   evidence it is *communicable*, not that it is *approved*. The PD ledger is fed by Founder
   rulings only.

# 1. Core brand

| Line | Flag | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **"Everything local. One Book."** | 🟢 | The anchor. Works as a wordmark line, an outro, a bio. |
| **"Find it. Book it."** | 🟢 | Shortest form. See the caveat below on the word *book*. |
| "Local services, without the group-chat guesswork." | 🟢 | |
| "The place your city's talent actually lives." | 🟢 | Aspirational without claiming scale. |
| "One app. Everybody local." | 🟢 | |
| "Everybody provides something. Everybody needs something." | 🟢 | Doubles as the one-account thesis. |

**Caveat on the word "book".** Today a booking is a **request** — the provider accepts it; it
is not an instant confirmation and no payment is taken. "Find it. Book it." is fine as a
brand line. **"Instantly booked", "guaranteed slot", or "pay in seconds" are not.**

**Brand voice, for anyone writing new lines:** plain, direct, unhyped. Short sentences.
Nothing that sounds like a pitch deck. The product is for people who work with their hands
and people who need them — condescension and start-up vocabulary both read as outsider.

# 5. Available Right Now / last-minute openings

> ### 🟡 **THIS ENTIRE SECTION IS PLANNED. DO NOT PROMISE.**
>
> **Find Me Someone Today** exists in-app as a **preview / coming-soon screen**
> (`app/preview/find-today.tsx`) that collects an interest signal. Same-day matching and a
> real "open right now" surface are **not built**. Every line below must be phrased as
> something being built, never as something available.

| Line | Flag |
|---|---|
| **"Someone canceled their 3 PM. You need a 3 PM. Why don't you know about each other?"** | 🟡 |
| **"An empty appointment doesn't have to stay empty."** | 🟡 |
| **"Last-minute doesn't have to mean impossible."** | 🟡 |
| "A cancellation is a loss for one person and a solution for another." | 🟡 |
| "Empty chair. Empty calendar. Same city." | 🟡 |
| "Somebody within five miles of you is free right now and you'll never know." | 🟡 |

**Why this concept is strong enough to be worth waiting for:** it is the only one in the bank
that creates value out of something that currently just *evaporates*. A cancelled slot is a
pure loss today. That is a genuinely new argument, not a better version of an existing one —
which is exactly why it must not be burned on a promise the product cannot yet keep.

**Safe framings while 🟡:** "This is the thing I most want to build next." · "Tell me if
you'd use this." · "Here's what an empty 3 PM costs a barber, and what I think we can do
about it."

# 7. Trust & verification

> ### ⚠️ THE MOST DANGEROUS SECTION IN THE BANK
>
> Identity verification is **PARTIAL — CORE SAFETY REQUIREMENT**
> ([BETA_SCOPE.md](../product/BETA_SCOPE.md)). Verification **state** exists; there is **no
> vendor, no ID collection, and no automated verification process**, and transactions are
> **not gated** on it during beta. **No marketing may state or imply that users are
> ID-verified.**

| Line | Flag | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **"Followers aren't the same thing as trust."** | 🟢 | A critique of the status quo, not a claim about us |
| **"Reviews should come from actual transactions."** | 🟢 | **True today**: reviews come only from completed Book transactions, are two-sided and blind, on a 7-day window |
| "No review without a booking. That's the whole rule." | 🟢 | |
| "Both sides review each other. Neither sees the other's until both are in." | 🟢 | Blind two-sided reveal is real |
| "A five-star profile nobody can verify is just a nice-looking screenshot." | 🟢 | |
| "You can't buy a reputation here. You have to do the work." | 🟢 | Framed as review integrity — **not** as identity verification |
| "Verified identity on both sides." | 🔴 | **DO NOT USE.** Not true today |
| "Background-checked professionals." | 🔴 | **DO NOT USE.** No such process exists |

**The safe trust story today is the review model, not identity.** Reviews-from-real-
transactions is a genuine, shipped differentiator and it is enough. Reaching for identity
verification claims trades a true story for a false one.

# 8. Transaction & payment protection

> ### 🔴 **CONCEPT ONLY. PAYMENTS ARE NOT LIVE.**
>
> **PD-042** locks that the loop is proven before payments. Payments and deposits are
> **PLACEHOLDER / FUTURE**; mechanics are open in **OQ-040 … OQ-046**. The in-app
> **Protection Center** (`app/preview/protection-center.tsx`) is a **coming-soon preview**.
>
> **Vocabulary ban — see [§ 29](#29-words-we-dont-use).** Never *escrow*, *insured*,
> *covered*, *guaranteed*, or *trust account* for money handling. Not in a caption, not in a
> comment reply, not verbally on camera.
>
> Full reasoning: [FUTURE_PRODUCT_IDEAS.md § 2](../product/FUTURE_PRODUCT_IDEAS.md).

| Line | Flag |
|---|---|
| **"If you honor the booking, The Book protects you."** | 🔴 — the north star. Vision framing only |
| **"Your deposit shouldn't disappear just because your provider does."** | 🔴 |
| **"A deposit should protect both people."** | 🔴 |
| **"Stop sending deposits into the void."** | 🔴 |
| **"We're not going to make contractors finance somebody else's project."** | 🔴 — strongest founder-POV line in the bank |
| **"Protecting clients shouldn't mean punishing good providers."** | 🔴 |
| **"The Book isn't trying to pick sides. We're trying to make both sides act professionally."** | 🔴 |
| "Cash App and a prayer is not a payment system." | 🔴 |
| "The complexity should be our problem. You just tell us what happened." | 🔴 |
| "Rules first. Evidence first. Not whoever complains loudest." | 🔴 |
| "Nobody should have to front $900 in materials and hope." | 🔴 |

**How to use these safely.** All of them work in **building-in-public** and **founder-POV**
formats, where the subject is *how a marketplace should work* rather than *what this app
does*. Frame as a position, a problem, or a design question:

- ✅ "Here's the thing I keep going back and forth on: who should eat the cost of custom
  materials when a job dies halfway?"
- ✅ "I don't think a protection system should mean the contractor finances the client. Tell
  me if I'm wrong."
- ❌ "Book with confidence — your payment is protected."

# 10. Barter

**🟢 SHIPPED and genuinely differentiated.** Real on `main`: barter offers, interests,
proposal/version negotiation, agreement finalization, two directed obligations per agreement,
delivery marking, one-time receiver answer, pre-delivery cancellation (either side, before
anything is delivered), a receiver-response window with Needs Attention, and receiver-reported
no-show routing to Under Review.

| Line | Flag |
|---|---|
| **"Your skill has value even when no money changes hands."** | 🟢 |
| **"Trade what you do for what you need."** | 🟢 |
| **"Two businesses. Two needs. One trade."** | 🟢 |
| "The oldest business model, with a receipt." | 🟢 |
| "A barber and an accountant walk into an app." | 🟢 |
| "Trades happen anyway. Now they're on the record." | 🟢 |
| "Both sides agree the terms before anybody does anything." | 🟢 |
| "Barter as a discount." | 🔴 | **DO NOT USE.** Barter is **not** a discount mechanism (**PD-031**) |

**Two hard limits.** Barter has **no effect on public reputation or ranking** in the first
beta — do not imply trading builds a score. And barter is **not** a way to get a cheaper
version of a paid service; framing it that way is the exact misuse the model was designed
against.

# 22. Recurring content series

The Founder-supplied series, plus format notes.

| Series | Concept | Flag |
|---|---|---|
| **Anybody know somebody?** | Open on the group-chat message everyone has sent. Cut to the answer being one search | 🟢 |
| **Available Right Now** | A real opening today, and who could use it | 🟡 — feature not built. Runs only as a *concept* series until then |
| **Trades That Just Make Sense** | Two businesses whose needs slot together. Barber ↔ accountant, photographer ↔ plumber | 🟢 |
| **Things That Make Me Add Another Feature to The Book** | Founder hits a real-world annoyance → cut to the fix. The bank's best organic format: it makes the roadmap feel earned instead of announced | 🟢 |

**Series discipline:** each has one job. *Trades That Just Make Sense* sells barter.
*Available Right Now* sells the cancellation-fill idea. *Why Is This Normal?* creates the
problem the others solve. Don't blend them — a series that means three things means none.

# 29. Words we don't use

| Word / claim | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| **Escrow** | Regulated term; licensing implications | "held until", "released when" — and only 🔴 |
| **Insured / covered** | Insurance is regulated; implies a policy | "protection" — and only 🔴 |
| **Guaranteed** | A promise we must honour in every unmodelled case | "protection", "what happens if" |
| **Trust account** | Implies a custodial arrangement | avoid entirely |
| **Verified / vetted / screened / background-checked** | No such process exists | say nothing about identity |
| **Instant / instantly booked** | Bookings are requests a provider accepts | "book", "request", "pick a time" |
| **Get paid faster** | Payouts depend on payments (not live) | nothing yet |
| **Rank higher** | Discovery ranking is **UNDECIDED** | "get discovered" — no mechanism implied |
| **Discount** (for barter) | Barter is not a discount (**PD-031**) | "trade", "no cash changes hands" |
| **Free** (unqualified) | A pricing commitment; the revenue model is **UNDECIDED** | "free during the Houston beta" |
| **Three strikes / auto-suspend** | Explicitly **not approved** | nothing |

# 30. How to add to this bank

1. **Write the line.** Don't self-censor at the idea stage — flags are the safety mechanism,
   not the drafting process.
2. **Flag it honestly.** Check [BETA_SCOPE.md](../product/BETA_SCOPE.md). If unsure whether
   something is real, it is 🟡 until someone confirms otherwise. **Defaulting to 🟢 is the
   only way this file becomes dangerous.**
3. **Drop it in the closest category.** Duplication across sections is fine; a line can be
   both a hook and a CTA.
4. **If it implies a product change, that goes in a product doc** — not here, and not as a
   Product Decision. See the workflow rule at the top.
5. **Never delete a 🔴 line for being unusable.** It becomes usable when the thing ships.
   Re-flag it then.
